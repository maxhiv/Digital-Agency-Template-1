Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

When do rankings improve?

Initial lift in 4–8 weeks; compound gains over 3–6 months.

What's included in technical SEO?

Crawl, indexation, Core Web Vitals, schema, internal links, and speed.

Do you create SEO content?

Yes—research → briefs → on-page optimization → publish.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

How is AEO different from SEO?

AEO targets AI/voice answers and snippets with structured Q&A and schema.

What changes go on my pages?

Q&A blocks, Schema.org markup, conversational headings, entity links.

Can AEO work without a blog?

Yes—service, FAQ, and location pages can still win answers.
